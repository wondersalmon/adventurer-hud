# Player HUD

[About the module](../README.md) · [GM HUD](gm-guide.md) · [Русский](player-guide.ru.md)

Select your character's token and press **Shift+R** to open or close the panel. With no token selected, the HUD opens your only available character or offers a choice.

The pin locks the panel's position and size. Escape does not close it by default; allow this in additional settings if you prefer. A closed HUD stays closed after reloading the page.

Outside combat, use checks, saves, skills, tools, inventory, spells and inspiration. Start rests from your character sheet. In active combat, the panel switches to weapons, spells, actions and features. Enable manual mode buttons through **⋮** if you prefer. Click the portrait or name to open the character sheet; use Tab and Enter on a keyboard.

Search helps you find an action. Favorites follow your character sheet, including modern Tidy sheets. Filter skills by proficiency and spells by preparation; remaining slots appear beside their levels.

Items and abilities use normal D&D 5e dialogs and resources. Items with several activities can offer a choice inside their card. **Shift** skips that picker and starts the usual item workflow. For rolls, **Shift**, **Alt** and **Ctrl** work where D&D supports fast rolls, advantage and disadvantage. The separate item button opens its sheet; **Shift** on that button posts its description to chat.

Click **HP** and enter a new total or a change such as `-7` or `+5`, with temporary HP separately. Death saves appear at 0 HP when needed. Hover over a condition for its description. On your turn, use **End turn** to pass play along.

If something breaks, open **Check integrity → Download error report** in the module settings. Do this before reloading: the file includes errors from this tab and version information to help troubleshoot.
