# Player HUD

[Module README](../README.md) · [GM guide](README.gm.md) · [Русская версия](README.player.ru.md)

A compact character panel for Foundry VTT 14 and D&D 5e 5.3+. It switches between exploration and combat automatically.

![Player HUD demonstration](media/demo.gif)

## Open the panel

Select your character's token and press **Shift+R**. Without a selected token, the HUD offers characters you own. You can enable a HUD button in Token Controls and **Open on login** in the module settings.

## Main actions

- **Exploration:** roll ability checks, saves, skills and tools; browse inventory and spells; use inspiration and rests.
- **Combat:** use Weapons, Spells, Actions and Features. Features use their configured D&D activity and consume its resources. The optional Skills tab has **Proficient and expert / All skills** filters.
- **Favorites:** manage favorites in the character sheet, including modern Tidy sheets; the HUD follows that list and hides the section when empty.
- **Spells:** switch between Prepared and All. Level headings and remaining slots stay visible as you scroll.
- **Health and conditions:** click HP to apply damage or healing, including temporary HP. Hover over conditions for descriptions. Death saves appear at 0 HP when applicable.
- **Your turn:** use End turn to advance combat when it is your character's turn.

## Shortcuts and settings

**Shift** gives a fast roll, **Alt** advantage, and **Ctrl** disadvantage where supported by D&D. Double-click the character portrait or name to open the sheet.
