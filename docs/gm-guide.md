# GM HUD

[About](../README.md) · [Player guide](player-guide.md) · [Companions](companions-guide.md) · [Layout editor](layout-guide.md) · [Русский](gm-guide.ru.md)

Enable **Use GM panel** in **Configure → GM panel**, then press **Shift+R**. The window menu opens settings and lets you switch to player mode.

![GM HUD](../media/gm-w.webp)

## Prepare an encounter

Choose the encounter, add creatures and roll initiative. You can add scene tokens or drag NPCs, actor folders and Encounters onto the creature list. Choose where new tokens should appear; cancel placement if needed.

Click an initiative value to roll or edit it. Preparation also offers initiative resets and participant removal. Removing a participant from the encounter leaves its token on the scene. Click **Start combat** when ready.

![Combat preparation](../media/gm-pre-fight.webp)

## Turns and creatures

**Follow turn** opens the current participant. Click another creature to inspect it manually; the current turn remains highlighted. **End turn** advances combat using Foundry's turn order.

Click a creature's name to open its sheet. Its controls let you ping or locate its token, change visibility and mark it defeated. Removing a defeated NPC can delete its scene token; check the confirmation before continuing.

Click **HP** for damage or healing; Shift-click restores full health. Hover over roster cards to see effects. Open action categories to use abilities, spells and resources through the usual D&D dialogs. Right-click a card for its sheet or Shift-right-click to send its description to chat.

## Customize the panel

GM settings let you choose selection behavior, visible controls and card details independently of player settings. Appearance options also apply to the GM panel and dice tray.

Use the pen to rearrange or hide blocks; see the [layout editor](layout-guide.md). The dice tray lets you choose who can see a roll.
